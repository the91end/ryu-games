# Sound credits

All recordings have been trimmed to 4 seconds or less, loudness-normalized,
converted to mono MP3 and renamed. Each one keeps its original license, listed
below. CC BY and CC BY-SA files may be reused if you credit the author and keep
the same license (for BY-SA).

The Wikimedia Commons licenses listed here come from the credits file of
[shiv19/animal-sounds](https://github.com/shiv19/animal-sounds/blob/main/assets-src/SOUND-CREDITS.md),
which is also where these files were taken from. If you redistribute a file,
check its license again on its Commons page.
